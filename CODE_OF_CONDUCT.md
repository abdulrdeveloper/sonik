# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Sonik to be a welcoming, respectful, and productive project for everyone. Contributors, maintainers, users, and guests should be able to participate without harassment or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Showing empathy and respect toward different opinions and experiences.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Communicating clearly and professionally.
- Respecting personal boundaries and privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately disruptive comments.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or project work.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate it. They may also take temporary or permanent action when appropriate.

Maintainers should act fairly, document decisions where possible, and protect the confidentiality of people who report concerns.

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainer through the repository's private contact method. Include:

- A short description of what happened.
- Relevant links, screenshots, or other context.
- The people involved, if known.
- Whether you would like follow-up.

Do not use public issues to report sensitive incidents.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and whenever someone is officially representing the project in public spaces.
