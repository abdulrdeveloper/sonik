# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Sonik is committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, and professional environment for contributors, maintainers, users, and community members.

We welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Participation in project spaces should be based on respect, good faith, and a shared interest in improving the project.

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Communicating with empathy, patience, and respect.
- Giving constructive feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Accepting different viewpoints and technical approaches.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Respecting privacy, boundaries, and consent.
- Keeping discussions, reviews, and issues relevant and professional.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith engagement.
- Publishing private or identifying information without permission.
- Intimidation, stalking, or unwanted contact.
- Plagiarism, impersonation, or knowingly misleading project communication.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, chat spaces, events, and any situation where a person is representing Sonik or its community.

## Reporting concerns

Please report harassment, abuse, security-sensitive conduct, or other violations privately to the project maintainer. Do not use a public issue for sensitive reports.

A report should include, where possible:

- A concise description of what happened.
- Relevant links, screenshots, or timestamps.
- The people involved or affected.
- Any immediate safety concern.
- Whether follow-up is requested.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation, or take other appropriate action in response to violations. Decisions should be proportionate, documented where practical, and applied consistently.
